# ROB Counts the Fireflies

## A note for grown-ups

This quiet counting story moves from one to five. Point to each glow and invite your child to count slowly. Fireflies belong outdoors, where little watchers can enjoy them without touching.

## One little light

![ROB notices one glowing firefly above a garden path at twilight.](images/rob-counts-the-fireflies-01.jpg)

The garden turned blue and sleepy.

Then—blink!

ROB saw one little light.

“Hello, one firefly.”

Hold up one finger.

## Two by the flowers

![ROB watches two fireflies glowing beside purple flowers.](images/rob-counts-the-fireflies-02.jpg)

Blink. Blink.

Two fireflies danced by the flowers.

One, two.

ROB watched with quiet blue eyes.

## Three by the gate

![ROB points gently toward three fireflies glowing above a low wooden garden gate.](images/rob-counts-the-fireflies-03.jpg)

Near the gate, another glow joined in.

One, two, three!

Three tiny lights made a wiggly trail.

Can your finger wiggle through the air?

## Four, then five

![Five fireflies form a loose glowing circle above ROB and a delighted small brown puppy.](images/rob-counts-the-fireflies-04.jpg)

The puppy came to look.

Four lights floated high.

Then one more blinked awake.

One, two, three, four, five!

Five fireflies. Five warm glows.

“Goodnight, little lights,” whispered ROB.

## Count together

1. Tap once.
2. Clap twice.
3. Blink three times.
4. Wiggle four fingers.
5. Hold up five fingers.

## ROB remembers

**ONE · TWO · THREE · FOUR · FIVE**

Counting slowly helps every little light be seen.
