# Building R.O.B. — physical edition plan

Physical copies are practical, but the current US Letter RGB PDFs are layout proofs rather than universal press files. Every service calculates its own wrap cover from page count, paper, binding, and spine width. Do not reuse a cover template between vendors or after changing the page count.

## Recommended proof sequence

1. **Start small.** Prepare one copy each of *ROB and the Lost Yellow Ball*, Volume 1, and the Complete Builder's Field Manual. This covers picture-book artwork, a youth workbook, and the longest/darkest technical edition.
2. **Choose the production intent.** Use a local printer or Lulu for classroom proofs and direct orders. Evaluate IngramSpark after ISBN ownership, bookstore terms, returns, wholesale discount, and distribution economics are decided.
3. **Request printer templates.** Confirm 8.5×11-inch trim, binding, paper, bleed, gutter, safe area, color profile, black generation, transparency, font embedding, and cover/spine dimensions.
4. **Export per vendor.** Convert only from the approved source edition. Keep interiors and wrap covers separate, and do not scale the current PDF at the press.
5. **Inspect physical proofs.** Check every page, especially dark photographs, fine cyan/amber lines, gutter content, captions, page order, trimming, spine text, and cover color under ordinary classroom light.
6. **Approve a short run.** Record vendor, template revision, file hashes, proof date, reviewer, and corrections before ordering inventory or enabling distribution.

## Practical routes

| Route | Best first use | Decision to make |
|---|---|---|
| Local commercial printer | Fast classroom/event proof and hands-on color discussion | Binding, paper, short-run quantity, and local pickup |
| [Lulu](https://www.lulu.com/) | Full-color print on demand and direct ordering | Product format, manufacturing cost, retail price, and shipping |
| [IngramSpark](https://www.ingramspark.com/plan-your-book/print/trim-sizes) | Wider bookstore and library distribution | ISBN, wholesale discount, returns, color format, and distribution margin |

The 235-page full-color manual will be expensive in print. Price it only after the vendor calculator and a physical proof establish manufacturing and shipping cost. A lower-cost classroom edition may use selected color signatures or split the manual into smaller volumes, but any redesign needs a new page and safety review.

## Release record

For each title, add the final trim/binding, printer template revision, interior and cover hashes, proof cost, unit cost, shipping, retail price, ISBN if used, proof reviewer, proof date, and approval result to the main release gate. No physical-sale claim should appear on the website until that record is complete.
