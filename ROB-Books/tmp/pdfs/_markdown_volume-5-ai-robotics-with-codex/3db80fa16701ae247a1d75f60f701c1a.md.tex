\markdownRendererHeadingTwo{14. What remains intentionally unfinished}\markdownRendererInterblockSeparator
{}Mature engineering is visible in the features a system refuses to fake.\markdownRendererSoftLineBreak
{}Cerebro reports general picking and arm motion as unavailable because the\markdownRendererSoftLineBreak
{}repository still lacks calibrated camera-to-arm transforms, complete neck\markdownRendererSoftLineBreak
{}mapping, joint feedback, inverse kinematics, collision checking, and a verified\markdownRendererSoftLineBreak
{}grasp executor. Gesture cues fail closed without a calibrated catalog and\markdownRendererSoftLineBreak
{}feedback-capable executor. Approval records operator intent but does not by\markdownRendererSoftLineBreak
{}itself start physical action.\markdownRendererParagraphSeparator
{}These are the next safe milestones:\markdownRendererInterblockSeparator
{}\markdownRendererOlBegin
\markdownRendererOlItemWithNumber{1}Establish measured coordinate frames and calibration artifacts.\markdownRendererOlItemEnd 
\markdownRendererOlItemWithNumber{2}Add feedback and freshness to joint state.\markdownRendererOlItemEnd 
\markdownRendererOlItemWithNumber{3}Build offline IK with joint and velocity bounds.\markdownRendererOlItemEnd 
\markdownRendererOlItemWithNumber{4}Add collision models and swept-volume checks.\markdownRendererOlItemEnd 
\markdownRendererOlItemWithNumber{5}Simulate and dry-run named motions.\markdownRendererOlItemEnd 
\markdownRendererOlItemWithNumber{6}Validate one arm on a bench at low speed.\markdownRendererOlItemEnd 
\markdownRendererOlItemWithNumber{7}Introduce a deterministic executor with stop preemption.\markdownRendererOlItemEnd 
\markdownRendererOlItemWithNumber{8}Only then expose a narrow, schema-constrained AI proposal tool.\markdownRendererOlItemEnd
