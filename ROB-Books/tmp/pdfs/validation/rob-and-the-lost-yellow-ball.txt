           A note for grown-ups
This is an imaginary story inspired by the real ROB. The pictures
are gentle illustrations, not build plans.
Read slowly. Let your child point, predict, count, and make ROB’s
sounds. Ask, “What should ROB do next?”
The repeating idea is simple:

       STOP • LOOK • THINK • CHOOSE • CHECK

ROB is a helper, but a trusted grown-up is always responsible for
a real robot.
